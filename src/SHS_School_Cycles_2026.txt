GHANA SENIOR HIGH SCHOOL (SHS)
ACADEMIC CALENDAR — 2025/2026 DOUBLE TRACK

This calendar applies to Senior High Schools (SHS) operating under
the transitional (double track) academic system in Ghana.

SOURCE: Official GES 2025/2026 Transitional Academic Calendar

=== SHS 1 — NEW STUDENTS ===
• First Semester (Term 1)
  - Reopening/Reporting Dates: 18th October – 20th October 2025
  - In-School Period: 18th October 2025 – 30th January 2026
  - Break/Vacation: 31st January – 15th March 2026

• Second Semester (Term 2)
  - Reopening Date: 16th March 2026
  - In-School Period: 16th March – 12th June 2026
  - Break/Vacation: 13th June – 2nd July 2026

• Third Semester (Term 3)
  - Reopening Date: 3rd July 2026
  - In-School Period: 3rd July – 4th September 2026
  - End of Academic Year: 4th September 2026

=== SHS 2 — SECOND YEAR STUDENTS ===
• First Semester (Term 1)
  - Reopening Date: 5th January 2026
  - In-School Period: 5th January – 17th April 2026
  - Break/Vacation: 18th April – 17th May 2026

• Second Semester (Term 2)
  - Reopening Date: 18th May 2026
  - In-School Period: 18th May – 4th September 2026
  - End of Academic Year: 4th September 2026

=== SHS 3 — FINAL YEAR STUDENTS ===
• First Semester (Term 1)
  - Reopening Date: 27th October 2025
  - In-School Period: 27th October 2025 – 13th February 2026
  - Break/Vacation: 20th December 2025 – 4th January 2026

• Second Semester (Term 2)
  - In-School Period: 14th February – 21st June 2026
  - WASSCE Exam Period: 20th April – 20th June 2026

• End of Academic Year: After WASSCE (21st June 2026)

=== NATIONAL KEY DATES & NOTES ===
• Shared reporting weeks:
  - SHS 1: 18th–20th Oct 2025
  - SHS 2: 5th Jan 2026
  - SHS 3: 27th Oct 2025

• National Breaks:
  - Christmas Break: 20th Dec 2025 – 4th Jan 2026
  - Easter Break: 1st April – 8th April 2026

• Proposed start date for 2026/2027 academic year:
  - 11th September 2026

END OF DOCUMENT
